What do the numbers in the first list have in common?

When a, b, and c are whole numbers, a quadratic factors exactly when D = b² − 4ac is a perfect square. Then √D is a whole number, so the x-intercepts are fractions or whole numbers. If D is not a perfect square, use the quadratic formula.
